Photo Collage Maker

Combine 2 to 6 photos into a single picture: a neat grid, a side-by-side strip, or a vertical stack. Before-and-after shots, product photos, birthday memories and comparison pictures all look better as one image.

Every photo is centre-cropped to its cell automatically, so faces and subjects stay framed without any manual editing.

How to use this Photo Collage tool

  1. Select 2 to 6 photos.
  2. Choose the layout: grid, side by side, or stacked.
  3. Press “Make Collage” and download the combined picture.

Private by design

Your file is processed entirely inside your browser, on your own phone or computer. It is never uploaded to a server, never stored, and never seen by anyone else — you can even use this tool offline after the page has loaded once.
