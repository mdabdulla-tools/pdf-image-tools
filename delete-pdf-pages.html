Delete Pages from PDF

Remove pages you do not need from a PDF — blank pages, duplicates, covers or mistakes. Type the pages to remove, such as 2 or 1,4-6, and a new PDF is created without those pages. Your original file is never modified.

Deleting pages is also a quick way to make a PDF smaller and cleaner before sharing it, without re-scanning or re-creating the document.

How to use this Delete PDF Pages tool

  1. Select the PDF file.
  2. Type the pages to delete, e.g. 1,4-6.
  3. Press “Delete Pages” and download the cleaned PDF.
