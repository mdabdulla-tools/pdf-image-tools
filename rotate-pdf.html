Rotate PDF — Fix Sideways Pages

Fix PDF pages that open sideways or upside down. Rotate every page, or only the pages you list (for example 1,3-5), by 90° clockwise, 180°, or 90° counter-clockwise — and the rotation is saved permanently into a new file.

Scanned documents often come out rotated. Instead of turning your head or your screen, fix the file once here and it will open the right way up on every device.

How to use this Rotate PDF tool

  1. Select the PDF file.
  2. Choose the rotation angle and, optionally, the pages.
  3. Press “Rotate PDF” and download the fixed file.

Private by design

Your file is processed entirely inside your browser, on your own phone or computer. It is never uploaded to a server, never stored, and never seen by anyone else — you can even use this tool offline after the page has loaded once.
