Image Cropper — Crop Photos to Any Ratio

Cut a photo down to the shape you need: a square for a profile picture, 16:9 for a video thumbnail, 9:16 for a story, or a classic 4:3 or 3:2. The position sliders let you choose which part of the photo survives the crop — left, centre or right, top, middle or bottom.

Cropping is lossless at the kept area’s original resolution: the tool cuts from your full-size photo rather than shrinking it first.

How to use this Image Cropper tool

  1. Select the photo.
  2. Choose a ratio and set the horizontal and vertical position.
  3. Press “Crop & Download”.
