Image Grid Splitter — 3×3 Instagram Grid

Split a single photo into 9 equal squares that form one big picture on your Instagram profile. The centre square crop is used, and the 9 images download in a ZIP, numbered in the exact order to post them: post-1 first, post-9 last.

Brands and creators use grid puzzles for launches and announcements because the full picture is revealed across the profile while each post still looks good on its own.

How to use this Image Grid Splitter tool

  1. Select a photo (square works best).
  2. Press “Split into 3×3”.
  3. Download the ZIP and post the images in number order.

Private by design

Your file is processed entirely inside your browser, on your own phone or computer. It is never uploaded to a server, never stored, and never seen by anyone else — you can even use this tool offline after the page has loaded once.
