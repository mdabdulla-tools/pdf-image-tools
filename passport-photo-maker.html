Passport / ID Photo Maker

Create an official-size photo from any selfie or portrait. Choose passport size (35×45 mm), US visa size (2×2 inch) or stamp size (25×35 mm) — the photo is placed on a clean white background at print resolution. You get the single photo plus a printable sheet of 4 copies.

Use the zoom slider to make your face bigger or smaller in the frame and the position slider to move it up or down, so head and shoulders sit correctly before you download.

How to use this Passport Photo Maker tool

  1. Select a clear front-facing photo.
  2. Choose the size and adjust zoom and position.
  3. Press the button to download the photo and the 4-copy print sheet.
