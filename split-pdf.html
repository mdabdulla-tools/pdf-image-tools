Split PDF — Extract Pages into a New PDF

Pull only the pages you need out of a long PDF and save them as a new, smaller document. Type a range like 1-3, a single page like 2, or a mix like 1,4,5 — the new PDF contains exactly those pages, in order.

Ideal for sending just one chapter of a book, one page of a contract, or the relevant pages of a report instead of the whole heavy file. Your original PDF is never changed.

How to use this Split PDF tool

  1. Select the PDF file.
  2. Type the pages you want, e.g. 1-3.
  3. Press “Split PDF” and download the new file.

Private by design

Your file is processed entirely inside your browser, on your own phone or computer. It is never uploaded to a server, never stored, and never seen by anyone else — you can even use this tool offline after the page has loaded once.
