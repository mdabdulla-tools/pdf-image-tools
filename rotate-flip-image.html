Rotate & Flip Image

Fix photos that came out sideways, or flip them like a mirror. Rotate by 90°, 180° or 270°, flip horizontally (left-right) or vertically (top-bottom) — then download the corrected image at full size.

A horizontal flip is also the quick fix for mirrored selfie text and logos, and a 180° rotation rescues photos taken with the phone upside down.

How to use this Rotate & Flip Image tool

  1. Select the photo.
  2. Choose rotate or flip from the list.
  3. Press “Apply & Download”.
