Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Your files stay on your device

Every tool on this website — PDF conversion, compression, merging, splitting, image resizing, cropping and all the others — runs entirely inside your web browser using JavaScript. The files you select are never uploaded to a server, never transmitted over the network, and never stored anywhere by us. When you close the page, nothing of your files remains.

No accounts, no tracking

We do not ask you to sign up, log in, or provide any personal information to use the tools. This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies to follow you around the web. The pages load a small number of open-source libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js and JSZip) from the jsDelivr content delivery network; that network may see a standard technical request for those library files, but it never receives your documents or photos.

Offline use

After your first visit, a service worker may cache the pages and libraries on your device so the tools load faster and can work offline. This cache contains only the website’s own files — never your documents.
